Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · 中文版

LyriStage is a personal, invite-only music player made by an independent developer. This page explains what information LyriStage collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with email, GitHub or Google, we receive your email address and, from GitHub or Google, your display name and profile picture. We never see your passwords for GitHub or Google.
  • Your library — songs you like, playlists you create, and your listening history (which songs you played and when). This powers your library, play ranking and daily recommendations.
  • Invite information — which invite code you redeemed.
  • Listen Together — while you are in a room, your display name, profile picture and what is playing are shared with the other people in that room. Rooms are not stored after everyone leaves.

Settings such as theme, language and lyric style are stored only on your own device.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your library in sync across your devices.
  • To build your daily recommendations from your own listening history.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Where it is stored

  • Supabase — accounts and library data, protected so that only you can read or change your own data.
  • Vercel — hosts the website and the music search service. Requests pass through it but your library is not stored there.
  • NetEase Cloud Music — song, album, artist and lyric information is fetched from NetEase. Your account information is not sent to NetEase.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, LyriStage only requests your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) to create and identify your account. This data is not shared with third parties and is used only for signing in. LyriStage's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can remove liked songs, playlists and listening history inside the app at any time. To delete your whole account and all of its data, email kengtingtan@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

隐私权政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 20 日

LyriStage 是一个由个人开发、采用邀请制的音乐播放器。这一页说明 LyriStage 会收集哪些资料、为什么收集，以及你可以怎么处理。

收集哪些资料

  • 账号资料：用邮箱、GitHub 或 Google 登录时，我们会拿到你的邮箱；用 GitHub 或 Google 登录时，还会拿到你的名字和头像。我们看不到你 GitHub 或 Google 的密码。
  • 你的音乐库：你喜欢的歌、你建的歌单、你的听歌记录（播了哪些歌、什么时候播的），用来显示音乐库、听歌排行和每日推荐。
  • 邀请资料：你用了哪个邀请码。
  • 一起听：在房间里时，你的名字、头像和正在播的歌会给同一个房间的人看到。大家离开后房间不会保存。

主题、语言、歌词风格这些设定只存在你自己的装置上。

用来做什么

  • 让你登录，并在你的电脑和手机之间同步音乐库。
  • 按你自己的听歌记录挑每日推荐。

我们不会出售你的资料、不放广告，也不会拿你的资料训练 AI。

资料存在哪里

  • Supabase：账号和音乐库资料，设有权限保护，只有你自己能读取和修改自己的资料。
  • Vercel：放网站和音乐搜索服务。请求会经过这里，但你的音乐库不会存在这里。
  • 网易云音乐：歌曲、专辑、歌手和歌词资料从网易云取得。你的账号资料不会传给网易云。

Google 用户资料

用 Google 登录时，LyriStage 只会要求你的基本资料（名字、邮箱、头像），用来建立和辨认你的账号，不会提供给第三方，也只用在登录上。LyriStage 使用从 Google API 取得的资料时，遵守 Google API 服务用户资料政策，包括"有限使用"的规定。

删除资料

喜欢的歌、歌单和听歌记录随时可以在 App 里删除。要删除整个账号和所有资料，请用你登录的邮箱写信到 kengtingtan@gmail.com，我们会在 30 天内删除。

政策变更

如果这份政策有更改，会在这一页公布新的版本和日期。
